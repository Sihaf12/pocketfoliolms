Leverage and margin

Lesson 2 of 3 in Risk and Protection. 12 minutes. Two of three to verify.

Leverage is borrowed size

You put up part of the position value as margin, and the venue lends the rest. Ten times leverage means you control ten dollars of exposure for every dollar you have posted.

It multiplies both directions

A 10% favourable move at 10x roughly doubles your margin. A 10% adverse move at 10x roughly erases it. The mechanism has no preference for which way it works.

The margin call is not a warning

When your equity falls below the level the venue requires, the position is closed for you, at whatever price the market offers. You do not get to wait for the recovery.

In practiceBefore opening any leveraged position, work out the percentage move that would take you to zero. If that number is smaller than a normal day for the asset, the size is wrong, not the market.
